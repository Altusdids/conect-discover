How account deletion works

Last updated: 24 October 2026

You can permanently delete your Conect account from within the app. When deletion completes successfully, your profile and ordinary connected activity are removed from the active Conect database, and associated profile photos are removed from storage. Shared conversations and all their attachments are removed for both participants, including attachments sent by the other person; the other person's independent profile and gallery remain. A limited safety-report record may be retained as described below.

Delete your account

  1. Open Conect inside the Telegram account associated with your profile.
  2. Go to More → Settings & help.
  3. Select Delete account permanently.
  4. Read the confirmation, then type DELETE when prompted.

Do not proceed unless you are sure. The action is permanent and cannot be undone.

What is deleted?

The account-deletion workflow removes the profile, profile photos and photo records, interests, swipes/likes, matches, messages associated with those matches, blocks and profile visits. It also removes every attachment in conversations being deleted, including videos or voice notes sent by the other participant. Other members’ independent profiles and profile galleries remain intact, but the shared conversation and its media are removed.

Limited safety-report retention

To help investigate abuse, repeated harassment, scams or suspected underage use, Conect may retain a limited safety-report record when a linked account is deleted. The direct reference to the deleted profile is removed where possible; a surviving profile may remain linked when needed for a safety review. The record may include the report reason, user-submitted details, status and timestamps. Access is restricted to authorised moderation personnel, and details that are not necessary should be redacted. Resolved or dismissed reports are intended to be deleted no later than 12 months after their recorded closure date. Open cases are reviewed periodically and retained only while needed. This retention change is staged for testing and applies only after it has been deployed; until then, the live workflow may delete linked reports.

What may remain temporarily?

Backups, security logs or provider-level copies may not be erased at the exact same moment as live records. The operator is still documenting provider backup/log retention periods. The safety-report exception described above is intended to preserve only case information reasonably needed for moderation for the stated retention period; it does not mean that full account data is kept.

Deletion errors:

If the app reports a deletion failure, do not assume the account has been fully removed. Some photo files or database records may already have been deleted even if a later cleanup step fails. Contact the Conect operator using the official support/privacy contact once it is published.

Before launch

The operator must publish a working support/privacy contact and confirm backup/log retention and the treatment of safety reports. See the Privacy Policy.
